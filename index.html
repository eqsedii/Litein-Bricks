Built on good clay

Litein Bricks supplies high-quality red clay bricks from Litein, Kericho County. Whether you are putting up a family home, a boundary wall or a rental block, you get solid, uniform bricks at a clear price.

  • Quality red clay

    Durable, good-looking bricks that suit load-bearing walls, partitions, fences and gate pillars.

  • No money upfront

    Order first, check the bricks when they arrive, then pay on delivery. You only pay for what you accept.

  • Delivered to your site
